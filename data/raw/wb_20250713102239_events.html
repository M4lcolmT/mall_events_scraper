WHAT'S ON AT THE EXCHANGE TRX

Look forward to seeing something new every time you come to The Exchange TRX. Our calendar is packed with exciting events we know you'll love, because we chose them just for you. 

 

Must-see events

 

  • AURUM | Superman

    Superman is now screening in IMAX at Aurum Theatre. 

    Unwind in plush recliners, complete with snacks and drinks - all included with your movie ticket, from as low as RM70.

    Find AURUM Theatre on Level 3. 

     

    BOOK NOW

  • SEIBU | International Chocolate Fair

    SEIBU's International Chocolate Fair is happening now until 16 July 2025 at Concourse Level, SEIBU Food Hall.

    Explore the world’s finest cocoa creations from brands like Venchi, ROYCE’, Elit, Cafec, and more. A sweet experience you won’t want to miss!

  • Apple | Workshop: Reimagine Your Note-Taking on iPad with Ping Hui

    Transform how you organise your ideas with the Freeform app on iPad, using content creator Ping Hui’s artistic approach to note-taking.

    You’ll learn a dynamic way to jot down thoughts with Apple Pencil, upping your productivity with structure and aesthetics. Make your own calming and inspiring visual board for achieving goals at school, work or home.

    Bring your Apple devices or we’ll provide them. Find Apple on Levels 1 & 2. 

    BOOK NOW
 
 

More Events at The Exchange TRX

 

  • Apple | Workshop: Take Smart Study Notes on iPad with Chee Hee Seng

    Discover how to transform your note-taking for study success using iPad with TCM practitioner Chee Hee Seng.

    He’ll share how he turned his study routine into a creative process, working seamlessly across Apple devices. You’ll learn how to capture ideas in Notes, then build your own planner in Keynote to stay organised, boost productivity and more.

    Bring your devices or we’ll provide them. Find Apple on Levels 1 & 2. 

    RSVP NOW

  • AURUM | Watch Jurassic World Rebirth

    Jurassic World Rebirth is now screening in ScreenX at Aurum Theatre.

    Unwind in plush recliners, complete with snacks and drinks - all included with your movie ticket, from as low as RM50.

    Find AURUM Theatre on Level 3. 

    BOOK NOW
 
 
  • Attend a Today at Apple Session

    Apple's very first store in Malaysia offers a full lineup of their products and services. Receive exceptional service from knowledgeable team members and learn how to get the most out of your devices through free Today at Apple sessions.  

    FIND OUT MORE
  • Catch a Flick at Aurum Theatre

    Boasting the most luxurious cinematic experience in Malaysia, catch the newest blockbusters at Aurum Theatre. With a range of plush, thematic halls to choose from, you'll also be treated to gourmet bites and beverages every time you choose Aurum. 

    If you have time before or after the show, stop by Kissa Diner or Hokkaido Table for an indulgent meal.  

    You're in for an unforgettable experience with Aurum Theatre.  

     

    BOOK NOW
  • Pay a Visit to Beauty Galleria

    Located on Ground Floor, Beauty Galleria brings together some of the world's most sought after beauty brands in a sophisticated, dedicated space. Find brands like Malaysia's first Guerlain Ultimate Boutique, Malaysia's first La Prairie and Acqua di Parma nestled within the beautiful curves of Beauty Galleria.  

  • Explore MPH Bookstores

    Embark on your next literary adventure when you walk through the doors at MPH. Step into a haven brimming with captivating stories, from timeless classics to latest releases. 

    Unleash your inner bookworm at MPH on Level 2. 

     

     

  • The Exchange TRX is a world-class experiential lifestyle destination that sits at the heart of Tun Razak Exchange, Kuala Lumpur's international financial district. With over 400 of the world's leading retailers, The Exchange TRX blends together an immersive retail journey together with the beauty of nature through the 10-acre TRX City Park.
