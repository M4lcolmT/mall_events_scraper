Terms & Conditions for the TAG Heuer Formula 1 Giveaway

  • 21 Aug 2025
  • by
  • The Exchange TRX
 

The Finer Details Behind the TAG Heuer Formula 1 Giveaway

  1. The TAG Heuer F1 giveaway (‘the contest) is organised by The Exchange TRX Management and TAG Heuer Malaysia Management (LVMH Watch & Jewellery Malaysia Sdn Bhd) (‘management’) and runs from 21 August, 10:00AM until 31 August 2025, 10:00PM.

  2. Entries posted after the stated closing date – 31 August 2025, 10:00PM will not be accepted.

  3. The contest is open to anyone who wishes to participate over the age of 18.

  4. Participation in the contest is free of charge and no application fee is required.

  5. The contest will be run on The Exchange TRX’s Instagram account.

  6. To participate, participants must do the following:

    Step 1. Follow The Exchange TRX and TAG Heuer’s accounts on Instagram.

Step 2. Like the contest post.

Step 3. Participants must visit the TAG Heuer Formula 1 Exhibition at The Exchange TRX and take a picture with any part of the exhibition. 

Step 4. They must post a photo of them with any part of the exhibition to their social media account.

Step 5. They must use the hashtags #DesignedtoWin and #TAGHeuerxTRX.

Step 6. They must tag 3 friends in their post.

Prizes for this contest are as follows:
Grand Prize: 1 pair of tickets to watch the Singapore F1 from 3-5 October 2025.
Runner-up prizes: 3x TAG Heuer Lifestyle Jackets

  1. Eligible submissions will selected based on a combination of factors including, but not limited to, quality of image(s) posted, composition and caption.
  2. The panel of judges will comprise of representatives from The Exchange TRX management and representatives from TAG Heuer Malaysia.

  3. Decisions made by the panel of judges are final. Appeals to the decisions will not be entertained.

  4. Unless stated otherwise, the contest has no affiliation or partnership with any other business.

  5. One user may submit multiple entries.

  6. Each winner may only win one prize.
  7. Once selected, winners must provide these details (name, IC number, mobile number and email address) for verification.
  8. The winner of the grand prize must ensure they have valid travel documents.

  9. Personal information provided must be accurate and participants must be reachable. The management reserves the right to disqualify or forfeit the participation under the circumstances of misinformation.

  10. Personal information collected is for administrative purposes only and will not be shared with third parties.
  11. If selected winners do not respond to management’s communications after three attempts, management reserves the right to select a new winner in their stead.
  12. Selected winners will have a period of 1 week (from when they were first selected as winners) to confirm attendance for the Grand Prize and, if they are runners up, to confirm that they will receive their prize. If a response is not received from the winner within the week, the winner foregoes their prize and it will no longer be available.

  13. The Exchange TRX reserves the right to refuse entry or refuse to feature anyone whether or not in breach of these terms and conditions.
  14. TAG Heuer reserves the right to offer a different prize with the same value.

  15. Management reserves the right to disqualify and/or reject the entry if found not to be in compliance with the rules and regulations of this campaign.
  16. By participating, you have consented to include your submission on selected The Exchange TRX’s social media platforms.

  17. Management reserves the right to use the names and/or images of the participants as materials for the purposes of advertising, distributing, trade, display and/or publicity, without any prior notice to the participants. All participants shall not be entitled to claim ownership or other forms of claims or compensation whatsoever on any of the image submitted during the contest. Management reserves the right to extend the rights to use the images to selected companies for marketing purposes without prior notice to participants.

  18. Management reserves the right to cancel, terminate or suspend the contest with or without any prior notice and/or assigning any reason.

  19. Management reserves the right to change, amend, delete and/or add to the contest Terms and Conditions without prior notice at any time.

  20. Upon submission, the participant is deemed to have accepted the terms and conditions of this contest and the participant acknowledges that he/she has read and understood The Exchange TRX’s Privacy Policy and Personal Data Notice which can be found on The Exchange TRX’s website consents to the usage, collecting, recording and processing of his/her personal data for the purposes stated in such notice and in connection herein.
