TRX CITY PARK - THE CITY'S NEW GREEN HEART

Kuala Lumpur has welcomed a new gem to its cityscape: TRX City Park. This 10-acre rooftop park is not just any park; it’s an internationally benchmarked haven with a distinctively Malaysian experience and identity.

 
  • Play Cave

    Filled with nooks and crannies, the Play Cave is a one-of-a-kind play space that gives kids endless opportunities to climb and explore. It’s the perfect place for children to burn off some energy while parents enjoy a moment of respite over a cup of joe. 

  • Play City

    Inspired by traditional local architecture, the impressive Play City boasts a unique play structure of three distinct sections, with exciting and engaging challenges for toddlers and teens alike.

  • Water Play

    Interactive and sensory, our Water Play offers a perfect blend of education and entertainment, making it a hit with kids of all ages.

 
 

Things to do in the park

Lose yourself in this sanctuary in the city, with every convenience just steps away.

 
  • Browse Local Flora

    Walk through TRX City Park and take in the sights and scents of over 150,000 plants from 150 local plant species. There's nothing else like it in the city. 

  • Grab a Seat Park-side

    Visit any one of our 7 unique park eateries and make alfresco dining part of an enjoyable day. 

  • Look for Fish

    How many fish can you spot in the koi pond? Head to Inspiration Garden and take it slow - you'll find more fish this way.  

  • Go Dragonfly Spotting

    The abundance of plants at TRX City Park attracts plenty of colourful insect-life. How many different kinds can you spot? 

  • Unwind at Inspiration Garden

    Find a quiet spot and breathe in and out. Even better, take out a good book. You'll feel better, trust us. 

  • Count Stars Underneath the Play Cave

    But you'll have to wait for nightfall to see the magic come alive. 

 
 
 

A Place That Celebrates Local Biodiversity

Envisioned as a place that brings nature back to the city, TRX City Park is teeming with local plant, insect and bird life. The City Park will be the ultimate outdoor public attraction in Kuala Lumpur, incorporating carefully considered environmental design solutions, to reinforce the sustainability and ecological profile of The Exchange TRX.
