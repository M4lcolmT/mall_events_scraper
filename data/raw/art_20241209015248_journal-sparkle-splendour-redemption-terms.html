Redemption Mechanics:

Tier 1 – Spend RM1,000 (RM800 with UOB Credit or Debit Cards) in two (2) receipts at The Exchange TRX will be entitled to receive a LEGO® Desk Calendar*.

Tier 2 – Spend RM2,000 (RM1,500 with UOB Credit or Debit Cards) in two (2) receipts at The Exchange TRX will be entitled to receive Limited Edition WICKED Tumbler*.

Tier 3 - Spend RM3,500 (RM3,000 with UOB Credit or Debit Cards) in two (2) receipts at The Exchange TRX will be entitled to receive Rituals Holiday Gift Set*. 

Customer must transact using UOB credit or debit card to be eligible for the offer and card must be present during redemption for verification purposes.
