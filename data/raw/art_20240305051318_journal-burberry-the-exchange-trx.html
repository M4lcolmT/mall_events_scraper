A Look Inside Burberry's Newest Store in Malaysia

The store pays tribute to classic British craftsmanship

  • 20 Dec 2023
  • by
  • The Exchange TRX
 

Malaysia's newest Burberry can be found right here at The Exchange TRX. Opening its doors on 29 November 2023, it features an array of menswear, womenswear and accessories from Daniel Lee’s debut Winter 2023 collection – including seasonal checks and the recently redrawn Equestrian Knight Design.

The interior showcases unique wooden sculptures by artist Casey McCafferty alongside rugs from British textile artist Tom Atton Moore – reinforcing the brand's commitment to craftsmanship.

Find Burberry and their Winter collection on Ground Floor.
