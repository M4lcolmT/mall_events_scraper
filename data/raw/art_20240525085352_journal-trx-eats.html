Everything You Need to Know About TRX Eats

KL's newest food experience

  • 3 May 2024
  • by
  • The Exchange TRX
 

We're excited to introduce TRX Eats – a month-long celebration showcasing the finest food, drinks, and music in the new social heart of Kuala Lumpur. Expect specially curated tasting menus, engaging workshops, and live entertainment.

Join us every Saturday for Saturday Soiree, featuring live musical performances by the renowned DJ collective, Saturday Selects, and interactive dance classes ranging from Latin & Hip Hop to Disco, Tropical Groove, and Jazz. Saturday Soiree promises an exhilarating blend of music, dance, and gastronomy, extending late into the night.

Eat & Redeem 

From 3 – 26 May, guests who spend RM200 or more in a single receipt at any of our F&B tenants* will earn a turn at our gashapon machine located on level 1 near Union Bistrot. Up for grabs are a wide array of awards from a variety of sponsors ranging from practical, everyday items to luxurious indulgences.  

* Only applicable for purchases made on the same day at The Exchange TRX excluding Mercato, Seibu, Aurum, bill payments, reload cards and money changers.
