The Exchange TRX New Year's Eve Countdown Party

  • 14 Dec 2023
  • by
  • The Exchange TRX
 

Everything You Need to Know About our NYE Countdown Party

Get ready for an exhilarating New Year’s Eve celebration with our extraordinary lineup of artists!

Celebrate and sing your heart out as we showcase the sensational K-Pop artist B.I  as our headliner! Supporting B.I is LEO, making his first debut in Malaysia. Our second international act is none other than Clean Bandit, who will make their return to Malaysia, this time with Grace Chatto performing a live PA set. 

But that’s not all! We will be featuring local acts such as Airliftz, Lunadira, CLAUDIA, Shelhiel, Ashley Lau  and Bad Habits that will elevate the experience.

Here's how you can secure your spot in 3 easy steps:

  1. Download The Exchange TRX mobile app
  2. Sign up to our Lendlease Plus MY loyalty programme
  3. Click on the New Year’s Eve Countdown event page & RSVP!*

 

Hot Tip: Upgrade to Enthusiast and above to book a VIP ticket!*

*Spots are limited and on a first come-first served basis

EVENT GUIDELINES

Entry

  • Those without a valid ticket will be denied entry.
  • Tickets are non-transferrable.
  • Should the loyalty programme member be absent, entry will be denied.
  • IC/Passport checks will be conducted to verify age. Please bring along Photo ID with your name and date of birth.
  • Children under 12 are not permitted entry, and individuals aged between 12-17 must be accompanied by an adult.
  • Please note that there will be bag checks for security reasons.
  • Once you are cleared for entry you will receive a wristband. This will allow you to re-enter the premises again.

 

Prohibited Items

Please do not bring any umbrellas, firecrackers, flares, sharp objects, walking sticks (unless medically required), bottled water, helmets, weapons, lighters, matches, lasers, alcohol, drugs, cigarettes, vapes, drones and megaphones.

Please note that there will be security checks (metal detector scan and bag check). 

Power banks will be permitted at this event. 

Cameras are also permitted however please note that professional photography is not permitted. 

Storage will not be provided on-site. You will be required to dispose of these items before entering.

Organisers retain the authority to deny entry to any item not explicitly listed, if deemed potentially dangerous or disruptive to ensure a secure and enjoyable environment for all.

Helpful Tips

  • We encourage you to come prepared for the weather - bring a raincoat/poncho (as umbrellas are not permitted) and wear sunblock.
  • F&B options are available onsite & re-entry is permitted.
  • There will be bag checks at re-entry.
  • The designated entry point to the event is at the MRT plaza entrance.
  • You will be required to show your event ticket on The Exchange TRX mobile app to be admitted entry.


Surau

The nearest Surau are located in The Exchange TRX. There is one on level 4 on TRX City Park (behind Inspiration Garden) and the other one is on Concourse level which can be acessed via an alleyway next to Typo. 

Getting Here on New Year’s Eve

MRT
We highly encourage you to use the MRT to travel to and from the event due to anticipated congestion and limited parking availability. The Kajang and Putrajaya lines will be open until 1:30am on the day of the event. 

Drop-Offs
The designated car drop-off areas are the Luxury Drop-off point in front of the Seibu entrance and MRT Plaza.

Grab Pins
In the Grab app, you can look up the following pins to be dropped off near the venue:
