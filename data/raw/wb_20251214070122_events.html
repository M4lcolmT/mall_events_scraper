WHAT'S ON AT THE EXCHANGE TRX

Look forward to seeing something new every time you come to The Exchange TRX. Our calendar is packed with exciting events we know you'll love, because we chose them just for you. 

 

Must-see events

 

  • Christmas 2025 | Twirl Into Wonderland

    Christmas has arrived at The Exchange TRX and it's more enchanted than ever.

    From 21 November 2025 to 4 January 2026, let the magic of the festive season sweep you into a world filled with dazzling decorations, exclusive redemptions, and even a delightful carousel ride at Central Exchange.

    Whether you’re shopping, strolling or snapping photos, there’s wonder to be found at every corner.  

  • Louis Vuitton | Les Voyage des Lumieres Pop Up

    Louis Vuitton welcomes the holiday season with a refined pop-up inspired by its festive campaign, 'Les Voyages des Lumières'.

    Paying homage to the Maison’s rich heritage, the space features a faithful recreation of the founder’s family home in Asnières, now brought to life at The Exchange TRX on Ground Floor for this holiday season.

    Find it on Ground Floor near the Louis Vuitton boutique.

  • Christmas Weekend Performances

    Feel the joy of the festive season this weekend! From festive performances and enchanting parades to special appearances by Santa, this weekend is filled with Christmas magic for everyone to enjoy.  

    Saturday 13 December
    1pm | Central Exchange - Christmas Juggling Show 
    3pm | Central Exchange - Frosty Friends Celebration (will move to other parts of the mall from starting point) 
    5pm | Central Exchange - Christmas Performances by Kingdomcity  

    Sunday 14 December 
    1pm | Central Exchange - Christmas Ballet Dance
    3pm | Central Exchange - Santa Meet & Greet
    5pm | Dining Terrace - Santa's Winter Wonderland (will move to other parts of the mall from starting point) 

  • diptyque | A Night of Wax & Gold Pop Up

    Take a journey through scent and storytelling at diptyque's A Night of Wax & Gold pop up. Showcasing Archibald the cat and the festive Sapin candle, it's the perfect chance to dive deeper into diptyque's enduring legacy.

    Explore the pop-up from now till 14 December on Ground Floor, City Avenue. 

  • Coach | Holiday Pop Up

    Discover a world of enchanting holiday magic when you visit the Coach Holiday Pop Up.

    With photo moments, weekend giveaways and charming holiday style, Coach has what you're looking for this December. Pop up runs until 4 January 2026. 

    Find the pop up on Ground Floor in front of Coach. 

  • Radiant Korea Pop Up

    Discover new K-skincare and K-snack favourites at the Radiant Korea pop up happening at Urban Node from 1 December to 14 December. 

    Immerse yourself in this one-of-a-kind cultural experience which showcases emerging Korean brands in an interactive format where testing is encouraged. 

    Find it at Urban Node on Ground Floor (near MRT Plaza).

  • Lego Pop-up | Concourse Level

    Builders of all ages, get your LEGO faves and discover new sets to add to your collection from the LEGO pop-up on Concourse Level. 
     
    There's also a building area for kids to express their creativity and before you go, don't forget to get some pics with the LEGO figurines! 
     
    Find the LEGO pop-up on Concourse Level on Park Avenue. 

  • Avatar: Fire & Ash | Exclusive Experience

    Explore Pandora as you never have with an all new experience at Sunlight Plaza, TRX City Park. 

    Embark on an epic stamping journey and redeem coveted collectibles once you have finished your quest. And don't forget to snap a pic so you can enter the giveaway to stand a chance to win a pair of IMAX tickets! Click here for more details.

 
 

More Events at The Exchange TRX

 

  • Back to Hogwarts Pop Up

    Rediscover the magic as you explore Harry Potter themed memorabilia and collectibles.

    Find your house colours and display them proudly and make sure you browse the full range so you don't miss out on anything. Pop up runs until 14 December. 

    So don't be a muggle - stop by the pop up on Concourse Level, near H&M. 

  • TROVE | Treatsmas Activation

    Step into a world of Infinite Wonders at TROVE.

    Spin and redeem exclusive treats given at random! 

    From 17 November to 28 December 2025, one lucky participant will win a RM1000 shopping spree every Monday.

    Try your luck at TROVE on Level 1. 

  • 11:59 x Bollinger Countdown Party at Hokkaido Table

    Countdown to 2026 at Hokkaido Table's 11:59 x Bollinger Party for a night of bubbles, beats and celebration. 🥂✨

    🎟️ RM68 gets you entry and a drink of your choice, from cocktails to sparkling wine or Paulaner.

    🍾 Want the full experience? Their RM1,288 table package for 4 pax includes Bollinger Special Cuvée, a premium bottle of your choice and festive party essentials. 🎉

    Find Hokkaido Table on Level 2.

  • Sanrio Gift Gate

    The Sanrio Gift Gate Pop‑Up has set up shop! Find beloved characters like Hello Kitty, Kuromi, My Melody, Cinnamoroll and many more, and pick up a wide range of stationery, plushies and trinkets to satisfy your inner child. 

    Whether you're shopping for yourself or looking for a gift, there’s something here for every Sanrio fan.

    Find Sanrio Gift Gate on Concourse Level, City Avenue (in front of Padini).

  • Aesop | Festive Exploration

    A Game of Chance awaits in-store at Aesop. 

    Allow their consultants to provide expert help in finding tailored gifts for your nearest and dearest, including limited-release Gift Kits or other appropriate offerings.

    With all purchases over RM350, you will receive a Gift of Chance card—simply cross your fingers, scratch the film, and see which complimentary gift awaits, while quantities last.  

    Find Aesop on Ground Floor.

 
 
  • Attend a Today at Apple Session

    Apple's very first store in Malaysia offers a full lineup of their products and services. Receive exceptional service from knowledgeable team members and learn how to get the most out of your devices through free Today at Apple sessions.  

    FIND OUT MORE
  • Catch a Flick at Aurum Theatre

    Boasting the most luxurious cinematic experience in Malaysia, catch the newest blockbusters at Aurum Theatre. With a range of plush, thematic halls to choose from, you'll also be treated to gourmet bites and beverages every time you choose Aurum. 

    If you have time before or after the show, stop by Kissa Diner or Hokkaido Table for an indulgent meal.  

    You're in for an unforgettable experience with Aurum Theatre.

  • Pay a Visit to Beauty Galleria

    Located on Ground Floor, Beauty Galleria brings together some of the world's most sought after beauty brands in a sophisticated, dedicated space. Find brands like Malaysia's first Guerlain Ultimate Boutique, Malaysia's first La Prairie and Acqua di Parma nestled within the beautiful curves of Beauty Galleria.  

  • Explore MPH Bookstores

    Embark on your next literary adventure when you walk through the doors at MPH. Step into a haven brimming with captivating stories, from timeless classics to latest releases. 

    Unleash your inner bookworm at MPH on Level 2.
