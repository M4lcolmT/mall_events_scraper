Full Terms & Conditions

Redemption items are exclusively for The Exchange TRX shoppers.

Redemption Mechanics:

RM200  in a maximum of one (1) receipt at any F&B outlets at The Exchange TRX will be entitled to win a turn at gashapon machine.
