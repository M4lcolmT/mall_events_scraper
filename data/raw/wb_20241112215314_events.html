WHAT'S ON AT THE EXCHANGE TRX

Look forward to seeing something new every time you come to The Exchange TRX. Our calendar is packed with exciting events we know you'll love, because we chose them just for you. 

 

Must-see events

 

  • Estee Lauder Companies | Gifts of Wonder

    This November, pick up a gift for you and gifts for your loved ones when you discover the magic of thoughtful gifting at the Gifts of Wonder experience at Central Exchange.

    With exclusive offerings from iconic beauty brands including Estée Lauder, Tom Ford Beauty, Jo Malone London, Bobbi Brown, MAC, and La Mer, expect an interactive journey with exclusive activities, expert-led masterclasses, personalised gift sets and more.

    So don't wait, it's only around till 19 November - head to Central Exchange and usher in the festive spirit! 

  • Today at Apple | Make Digital Postcards in Swift Playgrounds with James Abela

    Workshop: Make Digital Postcards in Swift Playgrounds
    Friday, 22 November 2024
    3:00–4:30 p.m.

    Learn how to create fun interactive postcards using iPad and Apple Pencil with award-winning educator James Abela. James will guide you through coding in Swift Playgrounds, showing how easy it is to bring your creative ideas to life. Then use your new skills to create a Malaysia-inspired postcard ready to share.

    REGISTER NOW
 
 

More Events at The Exchange TRX

 

  • Alice Saves Wonderland

    Embark on your very own whimsical adventure with Alice Saves Wonderland! With immersive experiences, memorable characters and interactive storytelling, get ready to enter a world of stunning visuals and unforgettable surprises. 

    Early bird tickets are available for a limited time and tickets are going fast. Secure yours now!

    BOOK NOW

  • Today at Apple | Make Beats Anywhere with GarageBand on iPad with Steve Jackman

    Workshop: Make Beats Anywhere with GarageBand on iPad
    Monday, 25 November 2024
    3:00–4:30 p.m.

    Discover how to make beats out of everyday sounds with GarageBand on iPad. Join music educator Steve Jackman to explore the possibilities of musical play and find inspiration in unexpected places. You’ll learn how to capture and manipulate sounds in your environment and experiment with loops and samples to create your own original beat.

    REGISTER NOW

  • MPH | Meet & Greet Author Kim Jiyun (and Translator Shanna Tan)

    Everyone’s favourite cosy laundromat is coming to Malaysia! If you loved Welcome to the Hyunam-dong Bookshop last year, this one’s for you. Come join author Kim Jiyun and translator Shanna Tan in a conversation about cosy books and the comfort that can be found in a warm community.

    When: 24 Nov 2024
    Where: MPH The Exchange TRX

     

 
 
  • Visit Malaysia's only Apple Store

    Apple's very first store in Malaysia offers a full lineup of their products and services. Receive exceptional service from knowledgeable team members and learn how to get the most out of your devices through free Today at Apple sessions. 

  • Pay a Visit to Beauty Galleria

    Located on Ground Floor, Beauty Galleria brings together some of the world's most sought after beauty brands in a sophisticated, dedicated space. Find brands like Malaysia's first Guerlain Ultimate Boutique, Malaysia's first La Prairie and Acqua di Parma nestled within the beautiful curves of Beauty Galleria. 

  • Catch a Flick at Aurum Theatre

    Boasting the most luxurious cinematic experience in Malaysia, catch the newest blockbusters at Aurum Theatre. With a range of plush, thematic halls to choose from, you'll also be treated to gourmet bites and beverages every time you choose Aurum.

    If you have time before or after the show, stop by Kissa Diner or Hokkaido Table for an indulgent meal. 

    You're in for an unforgettable experience with Aurum Theatre. 

    BOOK TICKETS
  • Enjoy a Meal at Dining Terrace

    Take in the lively alfresco atmosphere of Dining Terrace and enjoy after work bites and happy hour at a range of vibrant bars and restaurants. Experience authentic Sri Lankan food at Cafe Colombo, traditional English fare at Elementary, Spanish at Las Meninas and more, all at Dining Terrace on Level 2. Look for the glass doors between HOKA and Chica Bonita! 

  • The Exchange TRX is a world-class experiential lifestyle destination that sits at the heart of Tun Razak Exchange, Kuala Lumpur's international financial district. With over 400 of the world's leading retailers, The Exchange TRX blends together an immersive retail journey together with the beauty of nature through the 10-acre TRX City Park. Discover retail reimagined at The Exchange TRX.
